# Crafting & Mining SFX — Free Sampler

24 original synthesized sound effects for prototypes, game jams, and released games.

## Contents

- UI: 4 sounds
- Loot: 4 sounds
- Mining: 4 sounds
- Crafting: 4 sounds
- Machines: 4 sounds
- Rewards & Warnings: 4 sounds


The pack includes one seamless two-second machine-hum loop; its filename ends in _LOOP. The other 23 files are one-shots. Trigger one-shots normally and loop the hum with your engine's loop setting. Test volume in your game mix; source peaks range from -9 to -3 dBFS. Mono assets can be panned or spatialized by your engine.

## Quality checks

Files were reopened after encoding and checked for count, PCM format, sample rate, bit depth, finite values, clipping, edge samples, excessive leading/trailing silence, identical files, and the loop seam. Inventory.csv records duration and peak level for every file. These checks do not replace listening in your own game mix.

OGG versions are not included. No reliable local OGG encoder was available for this release.
